Shape & harmony

Composite veneers

Explore adjustments to shape and proportion using tooth-colored composite resin, with a plan guided by your oral health and preferences.

What to consider+
  • Material applied and shaped directly on the tooth.
  • Maintenance and polishing are part of ongoing care.
  • Preparation, suitability and longevity depend on your individual case.

Detail & expression

Porcelain veneers

Ceramic restorations individually planned around your smile. Discuss shade, proportions and preparation with your dentist before deciding.

What to consider+
  • Custom-made ceramic restorations.
  • Preparation and appointments vary by clinical needs.
  • Long-term care includes hygiene and professional follow-up.

Alignment & balance

Orthodontics

A considered approach to alignment and bite. Explore the options that support both function and appearance, with a plan for ongoing follow-up.

What to consider+
  • Assessment of alignment, bite and oral health.
  • Treatment options and duration follow a clinical evaluation.
  • International patients need a coordinated follow-up plan.
Resin or porcelain?+

There is no universal best choice. The right material depends on your teeth, expectations, maintenance and clinical assessment.

GOOD QUESTIONS. CLEAR ANSWERS.

Before your next chapter.

How much does smile design cost?+

Your estimate depends on the treatment, number of teeth and clinical needs. Start with a free initial virtual consultation to discuss your options; an in-person assessment confirms the plan.

How much could I save?+

It depends on comparable quotes and your actual travel costs. Use the calculator with your own amounts. Savings are not guaranteed.

How do I choose between composite and porcelain?+

Discuss your oral health, expectations, preparation, maintenance and budget with your dentist. Neither material is the right choice for everyone.

How long should I stay in Colombia?+

Your procedure and follow-up needs determine the schedule. Confirm appointments before booking flights.

Is the virtual consultation free?+

The initial virtual consultation is free. Additional examinations and treatments are quoted separately. A virtual conversation does not replace an in-person diagnosis.

Are flights and accommodation included?+

No. You organize and pay for travel, accommodation and transportation independently.

What happens after I return home?+

Agree on a follow-up plan before treatment, including who to contact and how additional visits or care would be managed.

Is everyone a candidate?+

No. Suitability depends on your oral health and clinical assessment. Another approach may be recommended.

How do I get started?+

Use the consultation button to connect with Sonrisas Colombia and request your initial virtual consultation.
