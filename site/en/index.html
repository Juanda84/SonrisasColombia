Questions we
get all the time.

How much do veneers cost in Colombia?

As a reference, composite veneers start at USD 180 per tooth and porcelain at USD 420. Most smiles use 8 to 10 veneers. We send your exact quote in writing after your consultation.

How much do I save compared with Puerto Rico?

As a reference, 8 porcelain veneers cost about USD 6,000 in Puerto Rico and USD 3,360 in Colombia: around USD 2,640 less. Compared with the U.S. the gap is larger. Try the calculator with your case.

Is it safe to get veneers in Colombia?

Colombia is one of the most popular dental travel destinations in the Americas. What matters is who treats you: ask for your dentist's professional registration (ReTHUS), a written quote and a follow-up plan. We give you all three before you travel.

How many days should I stay in Colombia?

As a reference, 3 to 5 days for composite and 7 to 10 days for porcelain. We confirm your appointment dates before you buy your flight.

Is the virtual consultation free?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp, send your photos and we talk on a video call at no cost. The final diagnosis is confirmed at your first in-person visit.

Composite or porcelain?

Composite is more affordable and faster. Porcelain looks more natural, resists stains and lasts longer. In your consultation we recommend the one that fits your teeth and budget.

What if I need something after I get home?

Before treatment we agree on your follow-up plan: who to contact, which check-ups you need and how anything unexpected would be handled.

Are flights and lodging included?

No. You book your flight and lodging, and we give you your exact appointment dates so you can do it with peace of mind.

Which cities do you work in?

Bogotá, Medellín, Cali and Pereira. Choose the one that works best for your flights or travel plans.

Can anyone get veneers?

Not always. It depends on the health of your teeth and gums. If you need another treatment first, we tell you clearly.
