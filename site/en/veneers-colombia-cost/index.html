How much do veneerscost in Colombia in 2026?

Short answer: in Colombia, composite veneers start at USD 180 per tooth and porcelain at USD 420. An 8-veneer porcelain smile costs about USD 3,360, versus roughly USD 6,000 in Puerto Rico and USD 11,600 in the U.S.

What changes the price

  • The material

    Porcelain costs more because it is custom-made in a lab and lasts longer. Composite is shaped directly on the tooth.

  • How many teeth

    Most smiles use 8 to 10 veneers: the ones that show when you smile.

  • Your dental health

    If you need a cleaning, fillings or gum treatment first, it is added to the quote.

  • The team's experience

    A well-planned design avoids touch-ups and early replacements.

How to save without taking risks

  • Ask for an itemized written quote before buying your flight.
  • Compare the same material and number of veneers in every quote.
  • Check your dentist's professional registration in Colombia (ReTHUS).
  • Agree on a follow-up plan for when you are back home.
