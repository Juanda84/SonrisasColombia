Cali is warm, joyful and close to the Pacific. Same plan, same care, and between appointments you enjoy the world capital of salsa.

Why get it done in Cali

  • Warm weather

    Temperatures around 25 °C, much like what you know from the Caribbean.

  • Easy days off

    San Antonio, Cristo Rey and live music make free days easy to fill.

  • Relaxed pace

    A big city that is easy to get around, so going to and from your visits is stress-free.

Choose how you want
your smile.

Three paths, the same care. In your consultation we tell you which one makes sense for your teeth and your budget.

Composite veneers

From USD 180 per tooth

Reference: 3 to 5 days in Colombia

The fastest way to change your smile. Resin is shaped directly on your teeth, with little or no reduction depending on your case.

  • Results in just a few visits
  • Can be polished and touched up
  • Great for shape, size and small gaps

Porcelain veneers

From USD 420 per tooth

Reference: 7 to 10 days in Colombia

The most natural and longest-lasting option. Custom-made in a lab, they keep their shade through coffee, wine and time.

  • Natural tooth shine and translucency
  • Stain resistant
  • Last longer than composite

Orthodontics

Length depends on your case

Align your teeth and bite with Dr. Katherine Peña, orthodontist. Sometimes it is the step that makes veneers look perfect.

  • Bite and alignment assessment
  • Check-ups planned around your trips
  • Can be combined with veneers

Questions about Cali

How many days should I stay in Cali?

As a reference, 3 to 5 days for composite and 7 to 10 days for porcelain. We confirm dates before you buy your flight.

Can I go dancing after my appointments?

Of course. Just avoid very cold or very hard foods and drinks on the days your dentist tells you.

How much do veneers cost in Cali?

As a reference, from USD 180 per tooth in composite and from USD 420 in porcelain. Your consultation confirms the final price.
