Choose how you want
your smile.

Three paths, the same care. In your consultation we tell you which one makes sense for your teeth and your budget.

Composite veneers

From USD 180 per tooth

Reference: 3 to 5 days in Colombia

The fastest way to change your smile. Resin is shaped directly on your teeth, with little or no reduction depending on your case.

  • Results in just a few visits
  • Can be polished and touched up
  • Great for shape, size and small gaps

Porcelain veneers

From USD 420 per tooth

Reference: 7 to 10 days in Colombia

The most natural and longest-lasting option. Custom-made in a lab, they keep their shade through coffee, wine and time.

  • Natural tooth shine and translucency
  • Stain resistant
  • Last longer than composite

Orthodontics

Length depends on your case

Align your teeth and bite with Dr. Katherine Peña, orthodontist. Sometimes it is the step that makes veneers look perfect.

  • Bite and alignment assessment
  • Check-ups planned around your trips
  • Can be combined with veneers

Questions about Armenia

How many days should I stay in Armenia?

As a reference, 3 to 5 days for composite and 7 to 10 days for porcelain. We give you appointment dates before you book.

Can I visit the Coffee Park between appointments?

Yes, on a day without appointments. It's a great family plan while your treatment moves forward.

How much do veneers cost in Armenia?

As a reference, from USD 180 per tooth in composite and from USD 420 in porcelain. We send your exact quote in writing.
